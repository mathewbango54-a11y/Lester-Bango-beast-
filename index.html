Guide 1: How To Use Lester's Prediction

  • Tap All Leagues or pick your league
  • Look for PLAYING TODAY - really playing today
  • Only bet STRONG or VALUE. Skip WATCH and NO BET
  • Tap View Details - Check Form: W-W-W = Good, L-L-L = Skip

Guide 2: How To Place A Bet

  • Open Hollywoodbets / Betway, login
  • Search game: e.g. Arsenal vs Chelsea
  • If Lester says OVER 1.5 - Tap Over/Under > Over 1.5
  • If Lester says BTTS - Tap Both Teams To Score > Yes
  • If Lester says HOME WIN - Tap 1 (Home Win)
  • Stake R10 if wallet is R200. Tap Place Bet. Screenshot it.

Guide 3: What The Numbers Mean

  • HOME 50% / DRAW 20% / AWAY 30% = Home most likely
  • OVER 1.5 - 70% = 70% chance 2+ goals. Very safe.
  • OVER 2.5 - 45% = 45% chance 3+ goals. Risky.
  • BTTS - 50% = 50% chance both score
  • Expected Goals 2.43 = Expect 2-3 goals

Rule: If OVER 1.5 is 70%+, that's your safest bet.

Guide 4: Money Guide - Don't Go Broke

  • Wallet: R200 for the week
  • 1 Bet: R10 (5%)
  • Max per day: 2 bets = R20
  • Lose 2 in a row: STOP. Tomorrow is another day.
  • Win: Don't increase. Next bet still R10.

Guide 5: When NOT To Bet

  • You are angry / desperate / drunk
  • You just lost and want to recover
  • Game is NOT in PLAYING TODAY
  • Form is L-L-L
  • You already bet 2 today
